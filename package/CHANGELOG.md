# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- **Creature levels:** creatures spawn with up to 5 stars, more in later biomes, and some with a modifier (Armored, Quick, Fierce, Regenerating, Splitting) shown by the colour of their name. Their young can inherit it. Wild creatures above the star limit get a new roll, so worlds coming from StarLevelSystem settle down. Stays off while StarLevelSystem or Creature Level and Loot Control is installed.
- **Gentler loot from starred creatures:** half again per star instead of the game's doubling.
- **Crossbows stay loaded** when you switch to something else and back.

## 1.0.0
- **The Kraken Axe:** a one-handed frost axe you throw with middle mouse and call back to your hand, with an ice explosion on its overhead chop (Shift and middle mouse).
- **Fast swap:** switching weapons, shields, torches and tools, and sheathing with R, no longer slows you to a walk.
- **Attack cancel and the fast bow shot fix moved here from Potato Tweaks.** Your settings for them carry over the first time PotatoFighter starts.
